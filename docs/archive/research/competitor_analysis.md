# Research: Competitor Landscape

## Executive Summary
The market is dominated by "All-in-One" giants (CentralReach) and legacy tools (WebABLLS). There is a significant gap for a modern, lightweight, UX-focused assessment tool that is cheaper and more flexible.

## Detailed Findings

### CentralReach
*   **Type:** Enterprise Practice Management.
*   **Status:** "The Gorilla". Owns the ABLLS-R/AFLS IP officially.
*   **Pros:** Fully integrated.
*   **Cons:** Expensive, extremely complex, slow setup.

### Catalyst (Therapy Brands)
*   **Type:** Data Collection.
*   **Status:** Popular for daily data (ABC data, trial counts).
*   **Cons:** Weak on aggregate assessment reporting.

### WebABLLS
*   **Type:** The "Original" digital version.
*   **Pros:** Official.
*   **Cons:** Dated UX, largely siloed data.

## Legal & Privacy Implications
*   Many US-based competitors treat Canadian data residency as an "Enterprise" up-sell or don't offer it at all. This is a competitive advantage for us.

## Engineering Implications
*   **Migration:** Clinics coming from WebABLLS will want to import their data. A CSV importer is a high-value growth tool.

## Risks & Recommendations
*   **Risk:** Trying to build "CentralReach Lite" (billing, scheduling).
*   **Recommendation:** Stay niche. Focus *only* on the Assessment & Progress Visualization capabilities to start.
